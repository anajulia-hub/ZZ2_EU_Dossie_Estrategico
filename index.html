Bloco 02 · História da Empresa
Headline do bloco
FROM BRAZIL WITH PRECISION.

ZZ2 was born in 2019 out of a simple frustration: modern drivers deserved better technology than what factory systems offered — but upgrading a car's infotainment felt like a compromise. Either you lost original features, or you ended up with an aftermarket solution that never quite fit.

Our founders — engineers with a passion for automotive technology — built ZZ2 to change that. Every product we create is designed to integrate seamlessly into the vehicle's existing system, preserving the original look and feel while adding the connectivity modern drivers expect.

From a garage in Brazil to a global operation. From our first CarPlay kit to over 58 interfaces compatible with the world's leading brands. From 2 engineers to an international team serving over 1,800 dealer partners across the Americas, Asia, and now — Europe.

"Innovation drives us. Connectivity defines us. ZZ2 leads the way."
